Scene Audit: dependencies and duplicates

A filter connects duplicate textures and materials to where they are used.

Scene Audit ↗

Seeing redundant copies

Identical assets can be hard to spot by looking at a scene. Scene Audit connects textures and materials to their usage, while a duplicate filter brings matching assets together.

The synthetic sample scene contains two groups: textures and materials. Import settings were unchanged in this workflow; there is no automatic deletion.
