Unity / Audio / Editor tools

From an event to a sound

Scene Audio connects scene events to sound banks. The editor lets you choose a clip, adjust volume and pitch, then follow playback in the event log.

Routing stays within the scene that emitted the event. One audio manager per scene is recommended.

What the sample shows

A sample event triggers a short synthetic tone. The log shows routing and the start of playback; a screenshot cannot confirm audibility on a particular device. Master volume affects the whole application.

Captured in Unity on 13 October 2026 using a synthetic sample scene. No public package is available.
