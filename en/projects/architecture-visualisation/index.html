Unreal Engine 5 / Modelling / Lighting / Architectural visualisation

An architectural study

A personal architectural visualisation project for learning Unreal Engine 5. The scene centres on a house I modelled in full, alongside some props I created myself.

One house, different lighting

The project explores lighting at different times of day. The gallery includes daytime and night views, interiors seen through glass facades, and a comparison of several lighting conditions.

Scope of the contribution

The house and some props are original models. Other scene objects are not presented as my models.
