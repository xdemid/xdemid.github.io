Technologies / disciplines

Unity / uGUI / Procedural geometry

Progress along a contour

One uGUI component builds an indicator along a circle, rectangle, triangle, diamond, heart or custom closed contour. Size, thickness, gradient and progress are adjusted in the Inspector.

The gallery shows six shapes at 25%, 62% and 75% fill. The circle in this sample is stretched horizontally and appears oval.

Scope

The tool supports closed contours and UI masks. No public package is available; the gallery is not a web demo.

Captured in Unity on 13 October 2026 using a synthetic sample scene.
