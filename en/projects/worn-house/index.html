A story told through the environment

A personal UE5 project for practising level design and lighting. The house appears ordinary, but its objects and atmosphere reveal the darker side of its owner — a man who pretends to be an ordinary member of society outside his home.

Light as part of the story

The focus was atmospheric light and the feeling of being in the scene. Warm light sources and cool window light guide the reading of the space and its details.

Working with assets

Most assets came from the Marketplace. Models and textures were reworked for the scene. Some props, including the gramophone, were made by me; I do not claim authorship of every object in the environment.
