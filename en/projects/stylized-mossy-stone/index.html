Stone and moss

A study of a procedural stylised material: moss spreads across stone tiles and the spaces between them. The material was created in Substance Designer.

Presenting the material

The images were rendered in Blender. The material is shown on spheres and planes, with a separate image presenting its texture maps. This is a material study, with no claims of game integration or measured performance.
